Kimchi Fried Rice

Cooking Time: 15 min

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Kimchi
  • Egg
  • Green Onion

Instructions

  1. Stir-fry the kimchi.
  2. Add rice and mix well.
  3. Cook a fried egg.
  4. Place the egg on top and serve.

Pasta

Cooking Time: 25 min

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • Pasta Noodles
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Garlic
  • Olive Oil

Instructions

  1. Boil pasta noodles.
  2. Cook garlic in olive oil.
  3. Add tomato sauce.
  4. Mix with pasta and serve.

Pancakes

Cooking Time: 20 min

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients.
  2. Pour batter into a pan.
  3. Cook until golden brown.
  4. Serve with syrup.
