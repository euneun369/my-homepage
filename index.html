Kimchi Fried Rice

Cooking Time: 15 min | Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Kimchi
  • Egg
  • Green Onion

Instructions

  1. Stir-fry the kimchi.
  2. Add rice and mix well.
  3. Top with a fried egg.
  4. Serve hot.

Pasta

Cooking Time: 25 min | Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • Pasta Noodles
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Garlic
  • Olive Oil

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta.
  2. Cook garlic in olive oil.
  3. Add tomato sauce.
  4. Mix with pasta and serve.

Pancakes

Cooking Time: 20 min | Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients.
  2. Pour batter onto a pan.
  3. Cook both sides until golden.
  4. Serve with syrup.
